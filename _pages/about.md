I am an **environmental and urban economist**, currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher and Teaching Fellow at ERUDITE, Université Paris-Est Créteil**. I defended my PhD in Economics from Université Côte d'Azur (GREDEG, CNRS) in 2025.

My research measures the **environmental and health costs of economic decisions made by firms and workers**. I combine quasi-experimental methods with large administrative and geospatialised microdata, including the French National Health Data System (SNDS), matched employer–employee records (DADS) and a high-resolution database of weather and climate (Copernicus ERA5).

## Research areas

- **Air pollution, health and its social cost.** I estimate the causal effect of air pollution on health outcomes and healthcare expenditure, using individual-level data on 132 million beneficiary-months in France. This work provides a baseline for evaluating emission reduction policies such as low-emission zones (project APOCALYPSE, funded by the French Ministry of Health).
- **Remote work, residential choices and the environment.** I study how the post-pandemic relocation of remote workers reshapes housing markets, the value of natural amenities and local air pollution.
- **Competition and the environment.** I assess how anticompetitive behaviour affects environmental outcomes, showing for instance that the European truck cartel delayed the adoption of cleaner engines and generated substantial excess emissions and infant health damage.
